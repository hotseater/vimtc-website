Immerse yourself in the timeless truths of Scripture and embark on a profound journey of academic excellence and spiritual discovery with our Diploma of Biblical Studies Program at Victory International Ministry Training College. Designed for individuals passionate about deepening their understanding of the Bible and preparing for effective ministry, this program offers a comprehensive curriculum, experienced faculty, and a supportive community to guide you on your path.

Program Overview:

The Diploma of Biblical Studies Program provides a comprehensive exploration of the Scriptures and theological principles, equipping students with:

  • In-depth Study: Dive deep into the rich tapestry of Scripture through rigorous academic study, guided by experienced faculty members who are experts in their fields.
  • Biblical Interpretation: Learn foundational principles of biblical interpretation and hermeneutics, enabling you to accurately understand and apply God's Word.
  • Theological Framework: Develop a robust theological framework grounded in biblical truth, preparing you to engage with contemporary theological issues and challenges.
  • Practical Ministry Skills: Gain practical ministry skills and hands-on experience through internships, practicums, and ministry opportunities, preparing you for effective service in various ministry contexts.
