VIMTC is accredited through recognized religious accrediting bodies such as the Transworld Accreditation Commission. While the institution offers degrees that are acknowledged within the United States, these degrees are not government-accredited. As a result:

  • Federal student loans are not available for these programs
  • Credits earned cannot be transferred to government-accredited universities with high tuition fees
  • Degrees cannot be used for promotions in secular workplaces where accreditation affects salary or job advancement; including resumes without clarifying the non-government accreditation is not advisable

This does not diminish the value of our institution, but it does limit these specific areas due to clear financial and regulatory distinctions. The government allows for Religious Exempt degrees because religious education has a different focus and content. VIMTC operates under this category and is accredited accordingly, similar to institutions like Rhema Bible College in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

Most ministry education in the U.S. takes place under this Religious Exempt status. Many Christian leadership universities accept VIMTC’s curriculum toward Associate, Bachelor’s, and Master’s degrees without the need for additional coursework. Within church and ministry communities, our training is well-respected, largely due to the strong impact seen in our graduates.
