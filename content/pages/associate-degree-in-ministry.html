Prepare for a life of impactful ministry and service with our Associate Degree in School of Ministry at Victory International Ministry Training College. Rooted in Biblical truth, practical ministry training, and personal development, this program is designed to equip you with the knowledge, skills, and spiritual foundation needed to become a confident and effective servant-leader in God's kingdom.

Program Overview:

The Associate Degree in School of Ministry offers a comprehensive curriculum that integrates theological education with practical ministry training, including:

  • Biblical Studies: Explore the depth and breadth of Scripture through courses in Old and New Testament studies, biblical interpretation, and theological foundations.
  • Ministry Skills: Develop practical ministry skills and leadership abilities through courses in preaching, pastoral care, discipleship, evangelism, and church administration.
  • Spiritual Formation: Cultivate spiritual disciplines, personal character, and spiritual maturity through intentional discipleship, mentorship, and spiritual formation activities.
  • Field Education: Gain hands-on ministry experience and apply classroom learning in real-world ministry settings through internships, practicums, and ministry placements.
