Application Procedure

All applicants should complete the application in its entirety and submit to the Office of Admission and Records (download from Here). The application must be accompanied by the following documentation:

  • Application Fee
  • Standard Passport Photo (2)
  • Statement of Faith
  • State of Purpose (Christian Conversion)
  • Pastoral Recommendation
  • Official Copy of High School Transcript
  • Privacy Rights of Students
  • Application Fee
  • International students must submit their Application Package to their Directors.
