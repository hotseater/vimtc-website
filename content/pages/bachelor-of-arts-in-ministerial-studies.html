Venture on a comprehensive and transformative educational journey with our Bachelor Degree in Ministerial Studies at Victory International Ministry Training College. Grounded in Biblical principles, theological depth, and practical ministry training, this program is designed to prepare you for leadership roles in the church, community, and beyond, equipping you with the knowledge and skills to make a lasting impact in the Kingdom of God.

Program Overview:

The Bachelor Degree in Ministerial Studies offers a dynamic curriculum that combines rigorous theological education with hands-on ministry training, covering key areas such as:

  • Biblical Studies: Delve into the Old and New Testaments, biblical languages, and advanced studies in theology and hermeneutics to deepen your understanding of God's Word.
  • Ministry Leadership: Develop leadership skills essential for effective pastoral ministry, church administration, and community engagement.
  • Spiritual Formation: Foster personal and spiritual growth through intentional discipleship, spiritual disciplines, and a focus on character development.
  • Practical Ministry: Gain practical experience in ministry settings through internships, field education, and collaborative projects, preparing you for real-world challenges and opportunities.
